A GUIDE FOR LITTLE EXPLORERS

Carry a light.
Remember the way.

Start in the top-left corner and reach your home in the bottom-right. Move one tile at a time with arrow keys, WASD, the direction buttons, or a swipe on the board.

  • Let your memory lead. The lantern reveals nearby tiles. Walls block light. Everything beyond its glow disappears, even places you have visited.
  • Find your sword. From chapter 2, stepping on a snake without a sword ends the attempt. With a sword, walking onto it defeats it automatically. The sword lasts for the chapter.
  • Bring the house key. From chapter 4, find the golden key to unlock home. From chapter 6, use your sword to clear brambles.
  • Leave yourself a reminder. Press B or the pebble button to place one of five pebbles. Press again on that tile to pick it up. Fireflies are optional little treasures.

There is no fuel limit or time pressure. Retry keeps the same map. New journey creates fresh mazes. Your unlocked chapters are saved on this device.
